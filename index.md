**Exploring Victorian Freight-Related Road Activity**

VICFREIGHT is an R package for exploring traffic activity across Victorian monitoring locations, with an emphasis on heavy-vehicle movements, vehicle composition and reporting completeness.

## Explore Victorian traffic activity

The package combines prepared traffic observations, reusable analytical functions and an interactive Shiny dashboard.

The dashboard provides four areas of exploration:

- **Overview:** Explore monitoring locations and traffic activity.
- **Site comparison:** Compare traffic volumes across selected monitoring sites.
- **Traffic trends:** Examine daily and monthly traffic patterns.
- **Reporting coverage:** Investigate incomplete reporting and data availability.

## Data

The package includes a prepared snapshot of Victorian traffic monitoring data covering May to October 2026.

Source: [Victorian Government – Telemetry Traffic Counts and Classification](https://opendata.transport.vic.gov.au/dataset/telemetry-traffic-counts-and-classification).
